The ground there was tracked with wolf prints and littered with the bones of demon beasts. And looking up at the mountains, I could see cave after cave. As we drew near, I spotted the first wolf. Its fur isure all over, its limbs thick, its eyes blood red above bared fangs with saliva stringing from the corners of its mouth.

The moment it saw us, it crouched low into an attacking stance. I read it with my spirit eye. It was an azure main wolf, blue quality. At level 18, its strength and agility and defense only near 120, carrying bite, pounce, and windb blade.

The first one we met being a blue level 18 at around 120 gave me pause. If the whole pack ran like this, the few of us might not have the strength to clear them. Even so, my mind was set on raising my territory here, whatever it took.

So, this pack had to die. And at worst, I would spend a magic scroll. For now that was clearly not needed. I told the others to begin. Windsor said nothing and threw out a binding spell followed by a light arrow.

She caught the Azure main wolf off guard and though it slipped the binding spell, it did not slip the arrow which took it in the waist and drew an instant gush of blood. The wolf looed a long howl that rolled across the slope.

One azureine wolf after another began spilling from the other caves in every size until 20 or 30 of them had surrounded us and pressed in to attack. At that moment, the ground ahead dropped into blackness and swallowed every wolf. And from inside came only howls thick with rage, blood lust, and a threat of fear.

Dark Moon had moved, releasing her dark domain. I used the cover to chant quickly and summon three mystic ice puppets, and Windsor summoned five angels of her own. They were plainly weaker than the one from the day before, but more than enough for a mob of wolves.

I had already checked that the fresh 20 or 30 Azure main wolves were all blue quality, but generally level 10 to 13, few of them higher. A blue level 13 wolf sat around 75 points, easy enough to handle. Inside her domain, Dark Moon was already hunting them, well able to deal with wolves at that level.

5 minutes on, she stepped back out of the dark domain, shook her windbreaking blade, and looked ahead as the darkness thinned. 11 Azure main wolf corpses lay on the ground. A third of the enemy's strength cut down in those 5 minutes.

The surviving wolves saw their packmates bodies and threw back their heads to help, then fixed their devouring eyes on us and rushed in on all fours. The five angels of light and three mystic ice puppets went forward to meet them while Windsor and I loosed skills to keep the rest in check.

Even so, a handful broke through to us. Then, Huawa moved. She took up the bloodthirsty demon spear as red flames climbed her body and a layer of red armor sealed over her. 8 m. Her cute shape gone cold and bloodthirsty into the fire marshall god. She entered the pack almost unopposed.

A demonst among them. The bloodthirsty demon spear plunging into an azureine wolf and draining its blood at once. By the time she tore the spear free, the wolf was already dead, its fur gone dull, one thrust between entry and exit enough to end a life.

Before long, the wolves that had reached us were wiped out to the last, and I was a little startled watching it since this was the first time I had seen Huawa fight on land. At sea, she had always been badly restricted and could never fully lose her power.

Right. Then another howl split the air, deafening and heavy with force, and I turned toward the sound. A giant azure main wolf leapt from a cave mouth and howled up at the sky. Far larger than the rest, a single strand of purple mane on its forehead.

Its eyes were not blood red, but azure. And where the others were raving beasts, this one carried the calm of a thinking creature, a little like the gym mountain turtle. The moment the azureine wolf leader broke from the trees, I swept my spirit eye across it and read the whole thing.

It stood at level 18, a purple quality beast whose every attribute had already climbed past 160. It could bite and pounce, hurl a windb blade, and work a pair of wind arts new to me. I knew my own limits well enough, and nothing in my hands could trade blows with a creature like that.

So, I told Huawa the leader was hers to take, and left it entirely in her keeping. She hovered a moment in the air, that cold and lovely face of hers softening toward me with a slight smile, and it was as if a whole winter melted in an instant into sunlight.

Then she beat her wings and closed on the azure main wolf leader at once. The bloodthirsty demon spear in her grip and blood red flames curling around her whole body. The leader felt her strength in its pupils shrank to points, its body flinching on pure instinct before its mind even caught up.

It refused to meet her head on, spinning a green wind vortex under each of its four paws, so that it slid clean out of the path of her strike. Her spear found only empty air, and not a flicker of feeling crossed her face as she pursued.

The wolf kept its distance and threw the odd windb blade back at her to harass. Too cunning to be pinned. That cunning finally pricricked her temper, and she chanted three ropes of blood red flame out of nothing and sent them coiling after the beast.

It dodged and dodged until its speed bled away. And that was the opening she wanted, closing the gap with a bloodthirsty demon spear until the two of them were truly locked together.

On my side of the field, mystic ice puppet and Windsor's Angel of Light had begun grinding through the rest of the pack with Dark Moon slipping in and out to worry their flanks. The Azer Wolves thinned and weakened, and before long, the whole pack lay dead.

The leader watched every one of its kin fall, and the pain of it clearly gutted the creature, losing on its own front against Huawa, bleeding from fresh wounds by the second. It was a dead thing that simply had not stopped moving yet. Rage, it turned out, does strange work.

The wolf half lost its reason, and the same fury dragged something buried up out of it. Layer on layer of green wind vortex crawled across its body until the whole beast was wrapped in a spinning shell of it, and its fur began to shift color under the wind.

The green darkened toward purple. The beast swelled into a giant purple wolf. Its eyes turned to pale gold, and a dark crescent bloomed on its forehead. I had never once seen anything like it, and I read the creature again just to be sure I was not imagining it.

Purple moon demon wolf. Now, gold quality and level 19. Its attributes vaulted up near 190 and a purple moon demon eye added to the tally. What in the world was this? Leveling up in a fit of rage was one thing, but jacking up your quality on top of it struck me as a shameless lack of marshall virtue.

The old gym mountain turtle had told me once that lifting a creature's quality was brutally hard for any race. It was a breakthrough in its very level of life, one that took both the right chance and the right moment. To watch it happen to my enemy right there on both counts at once, was almost insulting.

I did not panic. Even so, the wolf could trade blows with Huawa now. Yet, one long look at the field told me she still held the upper hand, and killing the thing was only a question of time. She lost her patience soon enough, and her own body answered the change.

The flames around her turned from blood red to bright gold, her armor shifting to a gold plate chased with blood, red, and black flame patterns. It was so richly worked that the void around her rippled and shuddered.

The bloodthirsty demon spear left her hand and hung in the air, and the golden flame poured off her body into the weapon until the whole shaft burned gold. A flick of her hand launched it, and the spear tore toward the purple moon demon wolf, whistling as it split the air.

The beast felt death coming and knew that if it failed to slip this one strike, it would simply end. It grew the vortexes under its paws, spat wind blades and tornadoes to block, even fired twin purple beams from the crescent in its eyes, and none of it mattered in the slightest.

The bloodthirsty demon spear punched clean through the wolf's waist and abdomen and nailed it to the ground while it was still howling. Huawa slid back into her fire marshall god form, dropped to the earth and closed one small hand around the shaft.

Blood red patterns rose across her fair little fingers to answer the patterns burning along the spear, and the wolf's blood began to run up the weapon and into her. Fear flickered in the beast's eyes, though more than fear there was ferocity, and its wars weakened and weakened until its body shriveled, its fur went dull, and it breathed its last.

I let out a slow breath at that. A small unexpected turn, but no real damage done. And the whole thing was closed.
